Fire-Spread Simulation (src/env/fire_sim.py) — deterministic, NOT ML
States: Safe / Fuel / Threat / Blaze / Burned Out. step(wind_speed_mph, wind_direction_deg, humidity_pct) — weather as live args.
Spread rule: ignition probability per Fuel cell = base rate × flammability × directional slope factor × directional wind factor × road resistance × humidity suppression, combined across 8 neighbors.
Ember spotting (spot fires): Blaze cells have a wind-scaled chance of igniting a Fuel cell 4–12 cells downwind, bypassing adjacency/road-resistance — can jump roads at high wind (26/30 trials at 45mph, 0/30 at 5mph in synthetic test). Ties to the real Palisades Fire's documented containment-line jumps.
Validated: uphill spread ~35% wider than downhill; road corridor cuts crossing 4–5x; buildings ignite only from adjacent burning vegetation; water cells never ignite. A directional bug (slope/wind vectors computed backwards) was caught via numeric burn-centroid drift checks, not visual inspection alone, and fixed.
